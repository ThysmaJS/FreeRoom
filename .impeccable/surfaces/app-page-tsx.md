## Direction contract

THESIS: The amphitheater's tiered-row rhythm carries the grid, not a generic dashboard spreadsheet — refuses the flat checkbox-table look every booking tool ships by default.

OWN-WORLD: ESGI navy #001B40 ground/ink, cyan #00EAFF as the one lit-state signal (raised from the seven-segment-display challenger: state is drawn as deliberate light/absence, never a gray checkbox), yellow #FFDE00 reserved for a single live "now" marker. Pill controls, cyan glow shadow on focus/active/own. Roboto in two weights only, Regular + Black (raised from the civic-bureau-prospectus challenger: no in-between weight noise). Hairline dividers, generous card radius.

STORY: A student opens the grid, instantly reads which row (room) has a lit cell at the hour that matters, taps it lit, it's booked — cancels the same way, from the grid or "Mes réservations."

FIRST VIEWPORT: today's grid fills the viewport under a slim navy header; rows = rooms banked like tiers, receding subtly in tone top-to-bottom; the current hour column carries the sole yellow "now" rule; free cells read as filled navy chips, own bookings glow cyan, booked/past cells sit dim and unlit.

FORM: amphitheater tiered-row rhythm, assigned index 3 of 7 grounded candidates; raised by civic-bureau-prospectus (competitive) and seven-segment-display (competitive); seed key bda0d165.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
